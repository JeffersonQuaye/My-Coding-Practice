Creamy Garlic Spaghetti

Ingredients

  • 200g Spaghetti
  • 2 cloves of garlic (minced)
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt & Pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley (optional)

Instructions

  1. Boil the spaghetti in salted water until al dente.
  2. Sauté garlic in olive oil for 1-3 minutes
  3. Add cream and stir over low heat
  4. Drain spaghetti and mix into sauce
  5. Season with salt, pepper and parsley. Serve HOT
